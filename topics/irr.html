Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

While Cap Rate provides a single-year snapshot, the Internal Rate of Return (IRR) evaluates the true time-weighted profitability across an asset's multi-year holding horizon.

IRR solves for the exact discount rate that forces the Net Present Value (NPV) of all future cash flows (annual operating cash flow plus the final reversion / exit proceeds Net capital received upon selling the property at the end of the hold period, minus broker fees and loan payoff. ) to equal the initial equity investment.

IRR Mathematical Relationship:
0 = CF₀ + [ CF₁ / (1 + IRR)¹ ] + [ CF₂ / (1 + IRR)² ] + ... + [ CFₙ / (1 + IRR)ⁿ ]

Where CF₀ is negative initial equity, CF₁..n-1 are annual cash flows, and CFₙ includes sales proceeds.

Institutional private equity sponsors target levered net IRRs typically ranging between 12% and 18% depending on whether the investment profile is Core, Value-Add, or Opportunistic.
