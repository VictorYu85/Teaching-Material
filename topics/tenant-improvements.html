Leasing & Tenant Operations

Tenant Improvements (TIs)

Tenant Improvements (TIs) represent the capital a landlord commits to customize raw or vacated commercial space for a new tenant's specific operations.

TIs are typically structured as a dollar-per-square-foot allowance (e.g. $45/sq ft) or as a turnkey buildout Landlord assumes full responsibility and cost risk for constructing the space to an agreed architectural plan. .

Effective Rent Impact:
In a 10,000 sq ft office lease at $35/sq ft for 5 years:
• Gross Lease Revenue: $1,750,000
• Upfront TI Allowance ($50/sq ft): -$500,000
• Leasing Broker Commissions (6%): -$105,000
= Net Effective Landlord Take: $1,145,000 ($22.90/sq ft/yr)

Because TI checks are paid upfront before rent starts flowing, landlords must maintain dedicated liquidity reserves in their annual cash flow models.
