Real Estate Valuations

Determining the objective fair market value of commercial and residential property requires synthesizing three core valuation methodologies.

Unlike publicly traded securities with immediate tick-by-tick prices, real estate relies on appraisals grounded in sales_comps (comparable sales) Recent transactions of similar assets in the immediate trade area adjusted for square footage, age, and condition. , replacement construction costs, and income capitalization.

1. Income Approach

Values the asset based on the present worth of future rental income streams (Cap Rate & DCF).

2. Sales Comparison

Benchmarks price per unit or price per sq ft against verified recent market transactions.

3. Cost Approach

Estimates what it would cost to buy the land and reconstruct the physical improvements today.

In commercial underwriting, the Income Approach is prioritized because investors purchase the contractual earning power of tenant leases.
