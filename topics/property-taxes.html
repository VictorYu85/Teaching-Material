Property Taxes & Reassessments

Property taxes are typically the single largest recurring line item in a commercial property's operating expense budget.

When a property transfers ownership at a price higher than its current county tax valuation, the county assessor initiates a tax reassessment Adjustment of the property's taxable assessed value to reflect the recent purchase price, often substantially increasing future tax bills. .

⚠️ Underwriting Trap: Inexperienced buyers model historic property taxes rather than pro-forma reassessed taxes. In a $10M acquisition of a building previously assessed at $4M, real estate taxes can more than double in Year 2!

Under Triple Net (NNN) leases, tax increases are passed directly through to tenants. Under Gross leases, the landlord absorbs 100% of the tax shock, directly compressing NOI and cash flow.
