Leasing & Tenant Operations

Lease Timing & Delivery Requirements

Signing a lease agreement is only the beginning. The operational schedule that converts a signed document into rent payments requires careful coordination of construction and municipal approvals.

Tenants condition rent payments on the landlord achieving substantial completion The construction stage when space is sufficiently finished with a Certificate of Occupancy (CO) so the tenant can legally occupy for business. by a contractual target date.

1. Execution & Permitting (Weeks 1 - 6)
Architectural plan submittal, city building permits, contractor bids.
2. Space Delivery & Buildout (Weeks 7 - 18)
Demolition, framing, MEP installation, finishes, and final city inspections.
3. Rent Commencement Date (RCD)
Delivery of Certificate of Occupancy (CO); tenant begins paying contractual rent.

Failing to deliver space on schedule can trigger liquidated damages or tenant termination options, highlighting the critical link between leasing and property operations.
