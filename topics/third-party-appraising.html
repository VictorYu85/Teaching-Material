Valuation & Underwriting

3rd Party Appraising

Before any institutional lender will issue financing on a commercial property, they commission an independent, certified 3rd party MAI appraisal.

The appraiser acts as an unbiased fiduciary to verify that the collateral value supports the proposed loan-to-value (LTV) ratio, protecting against appraisal gap When the independent appraisal concludes a value lower than the agreed purchase contract price, requiring extra equity or renegotiation. risks.

Key Components Evaluated by Appraisers:
  • Highest and Best Use (HBU): What legal, financially feasible use yields the maximum land value?
  • Market Rent Reconciliation: Are in-place rents above or below current market trends?
  • Replacement Cost Depreciated: Physical wear, functional obsolescence, and external economic drag.
  • Direct Capitalization: Selecting supportable market cap rates from paired sales comparisons.

An appraisal report provides banks with the legal justification needed to approve multi-million dollar loan commitments.
