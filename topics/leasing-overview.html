Commercial Leasing Fundamentals

A commercial property's value is directly derived from the contractual agreements executed between the landlord and occupying tenants.

The lease agreement establishes who pays for day-to-day property expenses, which is summarized through triple net (nnn) Tenant pays base rent PLUS all three operating expense categories: property taxes, building insurance, and common area maintenance. versus gross structures.

Full Service Gross (FSG)

Tenant pays one all-inclusive rent. Landlord pays all taxes, insurance, and utilities.

Modified Gross (MG)

Tenant pays base rent and pays increases in operational costs over a baseline year.

Triple Net (NNN)

Tenant reimburses 100% of property taxes, building insurance, and CAM maintenance.

Choosing the right lease structure shifts operational cost inflation risk either toward the landlord or onto the tenant.
