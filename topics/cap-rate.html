Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

The Cap Rate is the most pervasive metric in commercial real estate, translating an asset's Net Operating Income into an implied valuation.

Think of the Cap Rate as the inverse of a P/E ratio. It shows the percentage yield you would earn annually if you paid 100% all-cash for the property, eliminating financial leverage The use of borrowed capital (mortgage debt) to amplify purchasing power and returns on equity. from the comparison.

Cap Rate Formula:
Cap Rate = Net Operating Income (NOI) / Property Purchase Price

Property Valuation Formula:
Property Value = Net Operating Income (NOI) / Market Cap Rate

Key Rule: Cap rates have an inverse relationship with price. When cap rates compress (fall from 7% to 5%), property values increase. When cap rates expand, property values decline.
