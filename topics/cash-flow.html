Valuation & Underwriting

Cash Flow

Cash Flow is the true lifeblood of income-producing real estate. Unlike accounting profits or paper appreciation, cash flow represents the spendable capital deposited in your operating account.

When underwriting an acquisition, investors project whether the property can support positive monthly noi (net operating income) Gross revenue collected minus operating expenses (taxes, insurance, maintenance), before mortgage payments. after deducting ongoing operating costs and vacancy buffers.

Net Cash Flow Formula:
Effective Gross Income (EGI)
− Operating Expenses (OpEx)
− Debt Service (Principal & Interest Mortgage Payments)
− Capital Reserves (Replacement Reserves)
= Net Cash Flow (Before Taxes)

A deal with strong cash flow provides downside insulation during economic downturns, cushioning debt obligations even if market valuations fluctuate.
