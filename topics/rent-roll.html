Rent Roll Analysis

The rent roll is the definitive ledger of an asset's income. It documents who occupies each unit or suite, how much they pay, and when their leases expire.

Underwriters scrutinize the rent roll to uncover lease rollover risk The financial risk that major tenants will vacate at the end of their lease terms, creating sudden vacancy and leasing costs. and compare current in-place collections against prevailing market rates.
