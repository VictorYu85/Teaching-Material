Building Usefulness & Space Utility

A building's marketability depends not merely on gross square footage, but on its functional utility—how efficiently modern tenants can operate inside the physical shell.

Properties with poor layout or antiquated specs suffer from functional obsolescence A reduction in property desirability and rentability due to an outdated design feature that cannot easily or cost-effectively be remedied. .

Clear Height Industrial: 32' to 36' modern standard. Lower 18' clearances cannot accommodate high-cube automated rack systems.
Column Grid Spacing Wider column spacing (50' x 50') allows forklift maneuverability and flexible open-plan office layouts.
Loading Docks & Yards Adequate truck aprons (130'+) and dock-high doors per square foot drive logistics tenant demand.

Buildings with high space utility achieve premium lease rates and experience shorter downtime during tenant turnover.
